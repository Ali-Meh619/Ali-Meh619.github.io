About

Hi, I'm Ali! 👋

I'm a passionate researcher specializing in Large Language Models (LLMs), Graph Machine Learning, and Wireless Communications. My research interests include learning-based algorithm design for optimization problems, image processing, time-series analysis and forecasting, and Graph Neural Networks for traffic prediction and resource allocation in 6G wireless systems. I'm currently working as a Full-time Associate Applied Machine Learning Specialist at Vector Institute. Feel free to email me! [My CV]
